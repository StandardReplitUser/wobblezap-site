Terms

Wobblezap is a game you play for fun. This build does not charge money. There is no live in-app purchase and no ad.

Zap Coins and Gems are items inside the game. A public build's shop only trades Gems you already earned for Zap Coins.

The Weekly Zap Cup ranks you against rivals generated on the phone. Those rivals are not people, and the board is not Game Center.

The save can be erased with Delete my data in Settings. That erase cannot be undone.
