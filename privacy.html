Privacy

Wobblezap stores your game on this phone. The save includes coins, gems, mission progress, settings, and weapon use. It stays on the device.

What this build does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account.
  • It does not show ads.
  • It does not use tracking, and it does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps.
  • Unity analytics collection is turned off in the game.
  • It does not sell your save.

Delete my data

Settings includes Delete my data. Confirming it erases the save on that phone: coins, gems, missions, and settings. It cannot be undone. There is no copy on a server to delete.

Children

The game is a cartoon jelly shooter. It does not collect a name, email, or precise location.
