Support

Need help with Wobblezap? Email jurbanstudio+wobblezap@mail.grokbot.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version. We usually reply within a few days.

Play

Drag to aim and release to shoot. The game works offline. How to play is in Settings.

Save

Progress stays on the phone. Settings, Delete my data, erases that save. There is no account to recover it.

Shop

A public build does not sell anything. It can trade Gems you already earned for Zap Coins. It does not charge a card.
